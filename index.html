About

Hey! I recently completed an MS in Computer Science at DePaul University. My work centers on 1 question: how do we keep AI systems reliable once they reach production?

At sensen.ai, I worked as a Software Engineer on Computer Vision systems for license plate recognition. I served as the company's sole Data Scientist. I built a model-evaluation framework for 26 live deployments and worked directly with on-site teams and clients to turn operational failures into measurable engineering problems.

My Master's Research carried that same reliability question into LLM systems. I built a multi-agent Planner-Critic-Fixer pipeline that detects and repairs errors at inference time. The work is currently under conference review.

That evaluation-first approach now guides ChargeBack, an AI agent for card-fraud disputes built around evaluation, observability and guardrails.
