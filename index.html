About

Hey! I'm Kalyan.

As a Software Engineer with 2 years of work experience at sensen.ai, I bring immense depth in SQL & Python, to be brief.

Being the only Data Scientist at sensen.ai, I had an immense pleasure of interacting with on-site stakeholders and clients, every other day. We had about 80+ clients and I loved handling every one of their Data Science requirements by myself. That lasted an impact on me and I started loving roles that actively tackle client's problems, interact with them and provide solutions based on statistical grounding. This made me grow into a beast in SQL, thanks to sensen.

One of the most significant achievements of mine was the model evaluation framework, which keeps the clients posted on their model performance. I designed this specially for parking sites and it went on to become a standard across sensen. Over 2 years, I had a great pleasure to study depths of Data and its role in shaping the AI systems. My graduate research studied the statistics of LLM performance in monitoring conditions and tried to indirectly quantify hallucination.

Having finished my graduation, I'm going to take on more clients and try to solve their problems based on true data.

On-site teams across 4 countries brought me the problems they were seeing. I wrote the SQL, deployed the changes and checked the results with them. Over 2 years at sensen.ai I delivered 300+ reports that clients could run themselves by choosing the dates, sites or cameras they wanted to see.

Billing was a good example. Every 2 weeks, staff counted tickets by type, looked up the charges and worked out how much to invoice. I put those calculations into the product. Nearly every client used the new report and preparing a billing cycle went from about 10 hours to 2 per client.
