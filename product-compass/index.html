        What changed when I checked again?

        I fetched the updated records separately and compared the result with a full pull. The incremental run read 155 records in 7 API requests. The full run read 1,159 in 14. Both produced the same issue data across the cohort.

        What I’d do first

        I’d investigate #58615 first. The tools work in PowerShell and fail inside the desktop chat. That gives the investigator a specific difference to check. For #58626, I’d first ask which task needs the local computer. Knowing that would tell us whether the cloud option actually helps.

        Could I find the blockers just by looking for words like “failed” or “stuck” in the title? I checked a separate sample of 24 reports against a reading of their descriptions. The rule found 9 of the 20 reported blockers and missed 11. Two cases were unclear. I’d use the shortcut to flag issues for a closer look, and keep reading descriptions before setting priorities.

        This is an independent case study. The 12 reports are a deliberately selected sample. The actions are recommendations. The issue data can tell me about reported problems, but it can’t tell me how many customers left or what a fix would save.
