Notes

Error stops, warn surfaces.

An error breach halts the DAG, so the warehouse keeps serving yesterday's correct data. A warn breach is logged and shown.

Bad rows are quarantined.

Bad trips (fares of -$300, dropoffs in 2098) move to silver.trips_quarantine with the rule that caught them. The run fails only past 10% of the month.

1 scan for the whole batch.

Every check compiles to a Spark aggregate, merged into one df.agg. Silver's 14 assertions over 2.9M rows cost about 2 seconds.
