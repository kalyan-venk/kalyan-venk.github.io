Workflow

One conversation with controlled actions behind it

  1. 01

    Find the charge

    The agent searches only the signed-in cardholder's transactions and asks for clarification when several records match.

  2. 02

    Score the transaction

    A PyTorch fraud model exported to ONNX evaluates amount, hour and merchant category signals.

  3. 03

    Apply server-side policy

    The 0.33 and 0.67 gates decide whether to refuse, escalate or file. Ownership checks and a unique constraint prevent unsafe filings.

  4. 04

    Leave an audit trail

    Every tool call records its inputs, result, start time and latency beside the conversation that caused it.

Evaluation

The workflow is tested as a conversation

Seven golden conversations replay the full dispute path and check database records plus tool traces. The cases cover refusal, human escalation, duplicate prevention, unsupported action claims, score secrecy and wrong-owner access.

Current work

The completed 0.74-AUPRC scorer is being integrated into the full workflow. Response grounding, CI gates and cloud deployment come next.
